03 STORY
FORTY-FOUR
A project born from rupture and silence.

Alone with guitars, machines, rhythms and words, the project gradually became a place to transform inner states into sound.

Each song is a chapter. Each chapter is a mental state.

Not a continuation.
Not a return.

Deathrock, goth rock and coldwave form the foundations, but the music remains above all personal: cold guitars, hypnotic rhythms, ritual tension and an inner narrative.
